# Name

# Summary

Tracks every item, coin and currency you loot, with gold per hour, sessions, sources and drop alerts, in a report you actually want to look at. Retail and WoW Forever.

# Description

**Know exactly what your farming is worth.**

My Loot History tracks every item, coin and currency you loot, and turns it into a report you actually want to look at. Type `/mlh` and see if this spot is still worth farming, without leaving the game.

## What it does

- **Gold per hour, live**: in the report and in a small HUD on your screen, paused while you're away or resting.
- **Every drop, remembered**: items, currencies and coins, with quantity, value, zone, date and the mob it dropped from.
- **Sessions you can compare**: last night's farm next to tonight's, with an hourly graph, filters and CSV export.
- **All your characters in one view**: or just this one, your choice.
- **Drop alerts**: a toast for a mount, pet, toy, an Epic or anything worth more than you set, saying when gear is a new appearance.
- **Tooltips everywhere**: hover any item to see how many you've looted and when the last one dropped.

Prices come from vendors, or from the auction house with [Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator) or [Auction Bourse](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auction-bourse) installed.

Retail and WoW Forever, from the same download.

`/mlh config` opens the settings, also found in the game's options (AddOns tab).
